# Changelog

## [0.1.1] - 2026-11-10

### Added

- Added support for Fabric on Minecraft 1.21.1.
- Added support for NeoForge on Minecraft 26.1.2.

### Fixed

- Fixed trapdoor placement sometimes missing a quick Alt press.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-06

### Added

- Place trapdoors vertically against walls with Shift to open left to right or Alt to open right to left.
- Support trapdoors added by other mods automatically.
- Keep vanilla placement when neither modifier is held.
- Keep textures consistently oriented across opening, closing, and both placement heights.
