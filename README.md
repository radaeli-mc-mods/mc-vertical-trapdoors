<p align="center" style="background-color: #F4ECD0; text-align: center;"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/radaelilucca/mc-vertical-trapdoors/main/docs/gallery/section-titles/what_it_is.png" alt="WHAT IT IS?" width="267"></p>

When attached to a wall, vanilla trapdoors lie flat. **Vertical Trapdoors** lets you place them upright instead, including trapdoors added by other mods. It adds no new blocks or items.

<p align="center" style="background-color: #F4ECD0; text-align: center;"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/radaelilucca/mc-vertical-trapdoors/main/docs/gallery/section-titles/what_can_it_do.png" alt="WHAT CAN IT DO?" width="375"></p>

### Choose the opening direction

**Place a trapdoor vertically and choose which way it opens.**

- Hold **Shift** while placing it against a wall to make it open from left to right.
- Hold **Alt** while placing it against a wall to make it open from right to left.
- Place it without either modifier to keep the vanilla behavior.

<p align="center" style="background-color: #F4ECD0; text-align: center;"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/radaelilucca/mc-vertical-trapdoors/main/docs/gallery/section-titles/how-do-i-use-it.png" alt="HOW DO I USE IT?" width="371"></p>

1. Aim at the side of a block where you want to attach a trapdoor.
2. Hold **Shift** or **Alt** and place the trapdoor.

> **Heads up:** Clicking a block's top or bottom keeps the vanilla placement, even while holding a modifier.

<p align="center" style="background-color: #F4ECD0; text-align: center;"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/radaelilucca/mc-vertical-trapdoors/main/docs/gallery/section-titles/requirements.png" alt="REQUIREMENTS" width="355"></p>

- **Minecraft:** 1.21.1
- **Loader:** NeoForge
- Install the mod on both the client and server.
